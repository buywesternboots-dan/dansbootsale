Privacy

Effective date: October 21, 2026. Dusty's Boot Finder is run by ATS20, LLC (Dan's Boot Sale). Questions or requests: dansbootsale@gmail.com.

“My Boots” (your saved searches, ♥ picks, seen and hidden listings, and recent searches) is stored only on your own device, in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts, and none of it is sent to us or stored on any server.

Dusty's Boot Finder is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay.

What is kept on your device, and for how long

  • Saved searches: the search settings you saved (filters and keywords), the name you gave each one, and when it last ran with how many new matches it found. Kept until you delete them or clear your data. Up to 20.
  • ♥ Picks: eBay item numbers you hearted, and when. Removed automatically after 6 hours.
  • Hidden listings: eBay item numbers you hid, and when. Removed automatically after 6 hours.
  • Viewed listings: eBay item numbers you opened on eBay, and when (for the “Seen” label). Removed after 30 days. Up to about 2,000.
  • Recent searches: the settings of your last 20 searches, removed after 30 days.
  • Settings: your current search settings and display choices (grid or list, hide seen), and the time of your last visit (for the “New since your last visit” label).

We never keep listing titles, prices, photos or seller names on your device, and never store links. Every time you look at your picks or run your searches, the listings are fetched fresh from eBay and each link is built at that moment. Results from “Run all my searches” are not saved.

These are the browser storage keys the tool uses: dusty_myboots_v1, dusty_prefs, dusty_refine, dusty_view_prefs, dusty_last_visit (and, for one browser session, dusty_prev_visit).

See, download or delete your data

  • Download: open My Boots and tap Download my data for a copy of everything above.
  • Delete: tap Clear my data (in My Boots or under the results). It removes everything this tool keeps on your device in one step. Deleting a single saved search, un-hearting a pick or “Show again” on a hidden listing removes just that item.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser settings also removes everything.

Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing for us to look up or delete on our side. If you have a question, email dansbootsale@gmail.com.

Searching

When you search, your filter choices (not who you are) are sent to this site's server, which asks eBay for matching listings and sends back the results. We don't keep a record of your searches tied to you. We don't use analytics trackers or advertising pixels. There are no email, text or push alerts.

Cookies

This tool sets no cookies of its own. It uses your browser's local storage as described above, which stays on your device.

Links to eBay

This demo does not use affiliate tracking. Listing and search links are ordinary https links on ebay.com. The page does not add tracking parameters, and it does not show an advertising notice.

Buying on eBay

Every listing opens on eBay in a new tab. Bidding and buying happen on eBay, under eBay's own terms and privacy notice. What eBay does with cookies on its own site is governed by eBay.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security. The page's main font is loaded from Google Fonts.

Changes

If this notice changes, the effective date at the top will change too.
